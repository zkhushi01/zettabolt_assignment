# Remote Work Zones — Talent Acquisition Guidelines
**Document ID:** HR-RWZ-001
**Effective Date:** January 10, 2024
**Department:** Talent Acquisition
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose
This document lists the geographic zones from which NimbusWorks Technologies Pvt. Ltd. permits full-remote hiring (i.e., employees who will never be expected to relocate to or regularly visit an office).

## 2. Approved Remote Zones
Full-remote hiring is currently permitted in the following zones:
- **Zone A:** Pune, Bengaluru, Hyderabad
- **Zone B:** Jaipur, Chandigarh, Kochi
- **Zone C:** Any Tier-2 city with a NimbusWorks-partnered coworking space (currently: Indore, Coimbatore, Nagpur)

## 3. Excluded Zones
Full-remote hiring is **not** currently permitted for candidates based outside India, or in cities not listed above, due to unresolved payroll and compliance constraints.

## 4. Distinction from WFH Policy
This document governs **new hire location eligibility** — i.e., where a candidate must be based to be hired as remote-first. It is separate from the WFH Policy (HR-WFH-001), which governs how many days per week an *existing, office-based* employee may work from home.

## 5. Review Cycle
The list of approved zones is reviewed quarterly by Talent Acquisition in coordination with Finance and Legal, and may expand as new zones are approved.
