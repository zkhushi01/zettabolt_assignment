# Work-From-Home (WFH) Policy
**Document ID:** HR-WFH-001
**Effective Date:** June 1, 2023
**Department:** Human Resources
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose
This policy outlines the eligibility criteria and guidelines for employees who wish to work from home on a regular or occasional basis.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
An employee is eligible to apply for WFH if they meet **all** of the following conditions:
- Completed at least 6 months of continuous employment
- Received a "Meets Expectations" rating or higher in their most recent performance review
- Their role is designated as "remote-eligible" by their department head
- They have a documented history of no more than 1 attendance policy violation in the preceding 12 months

## 3. WFH Frequency
Approved employees may work from home up to 2 days per week. Full-time (5 days/week) WFH requires additional sign-off from the department head and HR business partner, and is granted only in exceptional circumstances (e.g., medical accommodation, approved relocation).

## 4. Equipment and Reimbursement
Employees approved for WFH are provided a one-time home office setup allowance and are expected to maintain a stable internet connection at their own cost.

## 5. Review
WFH eligibility is reviewed every 6 months alongside the standard performance review cycle. Eligibility may be revoked if attendance or performance standards are not maintained.

## 6. Note on Scope
This policy governs eligibility criteria for the WFH arrangement itself. It does not define which specific cities or geographic zones support full-remote hiring — that is governed separately by the Talent Acquisition team's remote hiring guidelines.
