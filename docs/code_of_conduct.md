# Code of Conduct
**Document ID:** HR-COC-001
**Effective Date:** November 1, 2022
**Department:** Human Resources & Legal
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose
This Code of Conduct sets out the standards of behavior expected of all NimbusWorks Technologies Pvt. Ltd. employees.

## 2. Workplace Conduct
- Employees must treat colleagues, clients, and vendors with respect and professionalism
- Harassment, discrimination, and bullying of any kind are strictly prohibited
- Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to the reporting manager and HR

## 3. Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose confidential company information, client data, or trade secrets to any external party, during or after employment.

## 4. Use of Company Resources
Company equipment, software licenses, and systems must be used primarily for business purposes. Incidental personal use is permitted within reasonable limits.

## 5. Reporting Violations
Employees who witness or experience a violation of this Code should report it via the confidential Ethics Hotline or directly to HR.

## 6. Disciplinary Action
Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. In cases of termination for proven misconduct (e.g., harassment, fraud, breach of confidentiality), the notice period requirement may be waived entirely at the company's discretion, and the employee may be relieved with immediate effect following due process and a disciplinary committee review.

## 7. Insurance & Benefits Note
Employees should note that group insurance coverage (see Benefits Overview) was migrated to a new provider as of late 2023; any prior references to the previous insurer in older communications are outdated.
