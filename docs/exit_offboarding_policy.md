# Exit & Offboarding Policy
**Document ID:** HR-EXIT-001
**Effective Date:** August 1, 2023
**Department:** Human Resources
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the standard process and notice requirements for employees resigning from NimbusWorks Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## 2. Notice Period
Employees who resign voluntarily (not during probation, and not for misconduct-related termination) are required to serve a notice period of **60 days** from the date of resignation. The reporting manager may approve a shorter notice period at their discretion, subject to a knowledge-transfer plan being completed.

## 3. Resignation Process
- Submit resignation through the HR portal
- Reporting manager acknowledges and initiates the exit workflow
- Knowledge transfer plan created within 5 working days

## 4. Full & Final Settlement
Final settlement, including pending salary, unused earned leave encashment, and any bonus payouts, is processed within 45 days of the last working day.

## 5. Exit Interview
All exiting employees are required to complete an exit interview with HR, either in person or virtually, before their last working day.

## 6. Return of Company Assets
All company assets (laptop, ID card, access cards) must be returned on or before the last working day. Final settlement may be withheld pending asset return.

## 7. Note on Scope
This policy governs standard voluntary resignation. Notice periods for termination due to misconduct are governed separately under the Code of Conduct (HR-COC-001) and may differ from the standard 60-day notice described above.
