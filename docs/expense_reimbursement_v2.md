# Expense Reimbursement Policy (Revised)
**Document ID:** HR-EXP-002
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2024
**Department:** Finance & HR
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose
This document supersedes HR-EXP-001 and reflects updated reimbursement limits, adjusted for inflation and cost-of-living changes across NimbusWorks office locations.

## 2. Travel Expenses

### 2.1 Hotel Accommodation
Employees traveling for business purposes may claim hotel accommodation up to **₹4,500 per night**. Any amount above this limit requires prior written approval from the reporting manager.

### 2.2 Local Conveyance
Local travel (cabs, auto-rickshaws) during business trips is reimbursable up to ₹700 per day with valid receipts.

### 2.3 Meals
Meal expenses during business travel are reimbursable up to ₹1,000 per day, split across breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

## 3. Submission Process
All expense claims must be submitted through the Finance portal within 15 days of the expense being incurred, along with original receipts. This process is unchanged.

## 4. Approval
Claims under ₹5,000 are auto-approved upon manager sign-off. Claims above ₹5,000 require additional Finance team review. This process is unchanged.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol, personal entertainment, and fines/penalties are not reimbursable under any circumstances.

## 6. Revision Note
This revision raises the hotel accommodation limit from ₹3,000 to ₹4,500 per night, along with proportional increases to conveyance and meal limits, effective February 2024.
