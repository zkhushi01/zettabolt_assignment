# Probation Policy
**Document ID:** HR-PROB-001
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2023
**Department:** Human Resources
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the terms of the probationary period applicable to all new full-time employees at NimbusWorks Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## 2. Duration
The standard probationary period for all new hires is **3 months** from the date of joining. This applies uniformly across all departments and job levels, unless otherwise specified in the employment contract.

## 3. Review Process
At the end of the probationary period, the reporting manager conducts a formal review covering:
- Job performance against role expectations
- Adherence to company policies and code of conduct
- Overall cultural fit

## 4. Extension
In cases where performance is not yet satisfactory, the probationary period may be extended by up to 3 additional months at the discretion of the reporting manager and HR, with written justification.

## 5. Confirmation
Upon successful completion of probation, the employee is issued a confirmation letter and transitions to full employment status with all associated benefits.

## 6. Termination During Probation
Employment may be terminated during the probationary period with 15 days' written notice from either party, without cause.
