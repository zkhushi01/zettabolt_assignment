# Performance Review Policy
**Document ID:** HR-PERF-001
**Effective Date:** April 1, 2023
**Department:** Human Resources
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose
This policy outlines the performance review cycle and rating framework applicable to all employees at NimbusWorks Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## 2. Review Cycle
Performance reviews are conducted **twice a year**:
- H1 Review: covering January–June, conducted in July
- H2 Review: covering July–December, conducted in January

## 3. Rating Scale
Employees are rated on a 5-point scale:
1. **Needs Improvement**
2. **Below Expectations**
3. **Meets Expectations**
4. **Exceeds Expectations**
5. **Outstanding**

## 4. Review Components
Each review consists of:
- Self-assessment submitted by the employee
- Manager assessment
- Peer feedback (for employees at Senior level and above)
- Calibration session across the department to ensure rating consistency

## 5. Linkage to Compensation
Annual compensation revisions and bonus payouts are linked to the H2 review rating. A rating of "Meets Expectations" or higher is required to be eligible for the annual increment cycle.

## 6. Improvement Plans
Employees rated "Needs Improvement" or "Below Expectations" are placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) for 60 days, with a follow-up review at the end of the period.
