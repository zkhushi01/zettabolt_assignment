# Expense Reimbursement Policy
**Document ID:** HR-EXP-001
**Effective Date:** April 1, 2022
**Department:** Finance & HR
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose
This document outlines the rules for claiming reimbursement of business-related expenses incurred by employees during official travel or work duties.

## 2. Travel Expenses

### 2.1 Hotel Accommodation
Employees traveling for business purposes may claim hotel accommodation up to **₹3,000 per night**. Any amount above this limit requires prior written approval from the reporting manager.

### 2.2 Local Conveyance
Local travel (cabs, auto-rickshaws) during business trips is reimbursable up to ₹500 per day with valid receipts.

### 2.3 Meals
Meal expenses during business travel are reimbursable up to ₹800 per day, split across breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

## 3. Submission Process
All expense claims must be submitted through the Finance portal within 15 days of the expense being incurred, along with original receipts.

## 4. Approval
Claims under ₹5,000 are auto-approved upon manager sign-off. Claims above ₹5,000 require additional Finance team review.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol, personal entertainment, and fines/penalties are not reimbursable under any circumstances.
