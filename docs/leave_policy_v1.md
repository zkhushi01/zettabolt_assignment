# Leave Policy
**Document ID:** HR-LP-001
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2023
**Department:** Human Resources
**Status:** Active

## 1. Overview
This document defines the leave entitlements for all full-time employees of NimbusWorks Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## 2. Types of Leave

### 2.1 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to **10 days** of paid sick leave per calendar year. Unused sick leave does not carry forward to the next year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

### 2.2 Casual Leave
Employees are entitled to 8 days of casual leave per calendar year, to be used for personal matters requiring short notice.

### 2.3 Earned Leave
Employees accrue 1.5 days of earned leave per month of service, capped at 18 days per year. Earned leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days.

## 3. Leave Application Process
All leave must be applied for through the HR portal at least 2 working days in advance, except in emergencies. Sick leave taken without prior notice must be reported to the reporting manager on the same day.

## 4. Approval Authority
Leave requests are approved by the employee's direct manager. Leave exceeding 5 consecutive days requires additional approval from the department head.

## 5. Public Holidays
Employees are entitled to 10 public holidays per year, as per the calendar published annually by HR.
