# Leave Policy (Revised)
**Document ID:** HR-LP-002
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024
**Department:** Human Resources
**Status:** Active

## 1. Overview
This document supersedes all prior leave policies and defines updated leave entitlements for all full-time employees of NimbusWorks Technologies Pvt. Ltd., effective March 2024.

## 2. Types of Leave

### 2.1 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to **12 days** of paid sick leave per calendar year, up from the previous 10-day allowance. Unused sick leave still does not carry forward. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

### 2.2 Casual Leave
Employees are entitled to 8 days of casual leave per calendar year, unchanged from the prior policy.

### 2.3 Earned Leave
Employees accrue 1.5 days of earned leave per month of service, capped at 18 days per year. Earned leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days. This provision is unchanged.

## 3. Leave Application Process
All leave must be applied for through the HR portal at least 2 working days in advance, except in emergencies. This process is unchanged from the prior policy.

## 4. Approval Authority
Leave requests are approved by the employee's direct manager. Leave exceeding 5 consecutive days requires additional approval from the department head.

## 5. Public Holidays
Employees are entitled to 10 public holidays per year, as per the calendar published annually by HR.

## 6. Revision Note
This revision increases the sick leave allowance from 10 to 12 days based on employee feedback collected in the 2023 engagement survey. All other provisions remain unchanged from HR-LP-001.
