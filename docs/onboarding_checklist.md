# New Hire Onboarding Checklist
**Document ID:** HR-ONB-001
**Effective Date:** May 1, 2023
**Department:** Human Resources
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose
This checklist guides new hires and their managers through the onboarding process at NimbusWorks Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## 2. Pre-Joining (Day -7 to Day -1)
- HR sends offer letter and joining kit
- IT provisions laptop, email account, and access badges
- New hire submits ID proof, address proof, and educational certificates

## 3. Day 1
- Welcome session with HR
- Laptop and system access handover
- Introduction to reporting manager and team
- Review of company handbook and code of conduct

## 4. Week 1
- Role-specific training begins
- Buddy assigned for first 30 days
- Benefits enrollment (health insurance, etc.)

## 5. First 90 Days
- Employee completes mandatory compliance training
- 30-day check-in with manager
- 60-day check-in with HR

## 6. Probationary Period
New hires are subject to a probationary period during which performance and cultural fit are assessed. The standard probationary period at NimbusWorks is **6 months** from the date of joining, at the end of which a confirmation review is conducted with the reporting manager and HR.

## 7. Confirmation
Upon successful completion of the probationary period, the employee receives a confirmation letter and is eligible for full benefits including ESOP grants (if applicable to the role).
