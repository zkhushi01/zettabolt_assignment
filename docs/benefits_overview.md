# Employee Benefits Overview
**Document ID:** HR-BEN-001
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2023
**Department:** Human Resources
**Status:** Active

bge-base-en-v1.5

## 1. Health Insurance
All full-time employees are covered under the group health insurance plan underwritten by **SecureLife Insurance Co.**, effective from the date of joining. Coverage includes the employee and up to 4 dependents (spouse, up to 2 children, and one parent).

Sum insured: ₹5,00,000 per family per year.

## 2. Life & Accident Insurance
Employees are covered under a group term life insurance policy equal to 3x annual CTC, along with personal accident cover.

## 3. Wellness Benefits
- Annual health checkup reimbursement up to ₹5,000
- Gym membership subsidy of ₹1,500 per month
- Access to an Employee Assistance Program (EAP) for confidential counseling support

## 4. Retirement Benefits
- Provident Fund (PF) as per statutory requirements
- Optional National Pension Scheme (NPS) contribution matching up to 5% of basic salary

## 5. Other Perks
- Annual learning & development budget of ₹20,000 per employee
- Free breakfast and lunch at all office locations
- Childcare assistance program for employees with children under 6 years
